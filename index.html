The Sixth Player Road to State

The Mirza High School Titans were once a promising program. Another losing season put them last in Conference 1. Morale is low, the roster is short, and the students have stopped coming.

You are the new coach. Build a roster, a culture, and a gym that can climb from the bottom of Conference 1 to a state championship. All-state honors and a next level are possible for your players. Another quiet spring is possible too.

In this gym the crowd is the sixth player. At Mirza, that player has left the building.

  1. Put your name on the clipboard and open the season office.
  2. Learn the roster, Conference 1, and the program before tryouts.
  3. Inside the office, the menu or keys 1 through 6 move between the desk, roster, conferences, schedule, program health, and the full rules.
Season rules on this desk
  • The record starts at 0–0. Conference rank is a preseason projection, not a result.
  • Roster overall and program strength are different numbers. Program strength includes depth and the building.
  • The academic line is 65. A full varsity is 12 players. Mirza does not have 12.
  • School spirit is the crowd. Tryouts, practices, and games are not on this desk yet.
  • The season is saved in this browser and does not need a connection.
